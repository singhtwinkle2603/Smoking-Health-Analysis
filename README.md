# 🫀 Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze the relationship between smoking habits and different health risk factors.

The dashboard provides insights into patient demographics, smoking status, smoking duration, cigarettes consumed per day, cholesterol levels, hypertension risk, and gender-wise smoking patterns.

The main objective of this project is to transform healthcare-related data into meaningful visual insights that can help understand the potential health risks associated with smoking.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall number of patients in the dataset.
- Understand the distribution of smoking status.
- Compare smoking patterns across different genders.
- Analyze years of smoking across different age groups.
- Examine the relationship between smoking and cigarettes consumed per day.
- Analyze cholesterol and hypertension risk levels.
- Compare average patient age and BMI with the overall patient population.
- Create an interactive and easy-to-understand healthcare dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Patient Overview
The dashboard displays the total number of patients along with important average statistics such as:

- Total Patients
- Average Age
- Average BMI

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the dataset.

### 2. Smoking Status Analysis

The smoking status section categorizes patients into:

- Never Smoker
- Current Smoker
- Former Smoker

This helps understand the overall smoking pattern among the patients.

### 3. Smoking Status by Gender

A comparison of smoking status between **male and female patients** helps identify gender-wise differences in smoking behavior.

### 4. Years of Smoking by Age Group

The dashboard analyzes the number of years patients have been smoking across different age groups.

This helps identify age groups with longer smoking histories.

### 5. Cigarettes Per Day

The dashboard also analyzes cigarette consumption across different age groups to understand smoking intensity.

### 6. Cholesterol & Hypertension Risk

The dashboard provides a visual comparison of different risk categories:

- High
- Low
- Normal

This helps analyze cardiovascular health-related risk factors among the patients.

## 🔍 Key Observations & Insights

### 👥 Patient Overview
- The dataset contains **69 patients**.
- The average patient age is approximately **53.7 years**.
- The average BMI is approximately **29.6**, indicating that the patient population has a relatively high average BMI.

### 🚬 Smoking Status
- Patients are categorized into Never, Current, and Former smokers.
- The dashboard allows comparison of the proportion of patients belonging to each smoking category.
- Former and current smoking patterns can be used to understand the smoking history of the patient population.

### 👨‍⚕️ Gender-wise Smoking
- Smoking status varies between male and female patients.
- The gender-wise comparison helps identify differences in smoking behavior between the two groups.

### 🎂 Age Group Analysis
- Smoking duration changes across different age groups.
- Older age groups generally provide more opportunity for longer smoking histories.
- Cigarette consumption also varies across age groups.

### 🫀 Cholesterol & Hypertension
- The dashboard shows different cholesterol and hypertension risk levels.
- High-risk categories can be used to identify patient groups that may require greater attention.
- The analysis demonstrates how multiple health indicators can be monitored together rather than looking at smoking status alone.
